# Dragon's Clutch regulatory inquiry

Status: pre-deployment research packet, dated 2026-08-17.

These documents prepare a factual conversation with the U.S. Commodity Futures
Trading Commission about Dragon's Clutch. They are not legal advice, a legal
opinion, a request for authorization, or evidence that any agency has reviewed or
approved the project.

Nothing described here has been deployed. No market has been created, no funds
have been accepted, no user has traded, and no transaction-building client has
been released. The purpose of the inquiry is to learn the applicable path before
implementation becomes a de facto product decision.

## Use discipline

Before external submission:

1. retain U.S. derivatives counsel and have counsel redline the entire packet;
2. replace every bracketed placeholder and reconcile every factual statement
   against the then-current repository;
3. decide the proposed legal person, collateral profile, product scope, user
   eligibility, control/upgrade posture, and compensation model;
4. check every linked authority for amendment, withdrawal, litigation, or
   supersession;
5. identify separately any SEC, FinCEN, OFAC, state, tax, consumer-protection,
   privacy, or gaming analysis required;
6. do not treat an Innovation Task Force meeting as relief; and
7. do not deploy or solicit users until the resulting legal gate is explicit.

Formal CFTC staff relief under Regulation 140.99 is fact- and requester-specific.
A no-action letter binds only the issuing staff division; an exemptive letter has
a different effect; and third parties generally cannot rely on another person's
letter. Granted requests and responses are ordinarily published. Those limits
are described in the [CFTC's definitions](https://www.cftc.gov/LawRegulation/CFTCStaffLetters/lettersdefined)
and [relief-letter publication FAQ](https://www.cftc.gov/Transparency/relieffaqs).
