Two machines, one price

The disagreement

The claim under exhibit: an automated trader can state a precise belief — a density rather than a direction — and the market prices disagreement between models instead of only between moods. This page is that claim run, not argued: two named textbook estimators each emitted a basis-moment vector derived from a distribution, a published deterministic function lowered the pair of vectors into a clearing book, and that book placed, froze, cleared, and settled end to end through the general clearing plane of a local laboratory bank, with conservation asserted to the atom and the pot provably empty. The cleared price vector lies coordinatewise between the two beliefs, is checkably the basis-moment vector of an explicit probability measure, and carries a recomputable zero duality gap. No resolution ever occurs: the exhibit ends at the settled book, so neither model is ever “right.”

Two textbook estimators, pre-registered

The quantity is the 20-trading-day-ahead level of SYN-1, a synthetic price series published with the exhibit — no live data. SYN-1 is 120 daily steps from 160.0: each day’s log-return is drawn gauss(0, 0.01) and, with probability 0.10, a jump of ±0.06 is added — all constants published, seed 20260827 chosen by rule (the filing date) before generation. The realized terminal level is $156.48, with 4 realized jump days of 120. The horizon and grid were pre-registered before looking at output: H = 20; the Friday-clutch grid — knots $100 to $240 step $20, degree 1, eight hats, S = 10,000.

  • Model G, “the Gaussian”: a maximum-likelihood normal fit to SYN-1’s daily log-returns (μ = −0.00019, σ = 0.01477), projected 20 days as a lognormal over the terminal level, integrated against the clamped hats.
  • Model E, “the empiricist”: the empirical distribution of all 101 overlapping 20-day log-moves in the same history, applied to the terminal level, averaged through the clamped hats.

Honesty box. Each model is a named textbook estimator, one screen of code, zero free hyperparameters beyond the pre-registered rule. Both ran once on the same published history; neither saw a price or the other’s output; nothing was adjusted after generation. The generator’s jump term is declared design: jump variance inflates G’s fitted σ so G spreads mass into the shoulders while E keeps the realized peak — that structural split is what the exhibit needs, and it was chosen for exactly that reason. The book-former below is a published deterministic function of the two belief vectors and nothing else: the exhibit does not simulate order-flow discovery between autonomous agents; it lowers a known disagreement into the minimal book that expresses it. Estimation is float (disclosed); the two quantized integer vectors are pinned in docs/site-plan/disagreement_check.py, regeneration is checked against them (a differing platform libm is the named boundary), and everything downstream is exact integer arithmetic matching the venue.

The book a disagreement writes

The book-former reads the pair (v_G, v_E) and emits three things. Per-knot quotes: at each knot where the values differ, the higher-value model posts a buy of 500 Eggs at its own value and the lower-value model posts a sell at its own value (a zero value quotes a 0-limit sell); knots where they agree get no orders. The disagreement package: one portfolio pair with coefficients c⁺ = Δ⁺ = [0, 0, 299, 0, 0, 32, 0, 0] — one lot is the per-mille excess of G’s belief over E’s. G posts the 50-lot buy at ⌊dot(c⁺,v_G)/S⌋ = 88 atoms per lot, E the matching sell at ⌈dot(c⁺,v_E)/S⌉ = 80. The trade window (80, 88) is open because and only because the models disagree: dot(c⁺,v_G) − dot(c⁺,v_E) = ‖Δ⁺‖² = 90,425 > 0 — the room to trade is the squared disagreement. And one uncrossed quote for refusal texture: E posts a second, lower buy at the $120 knot (limit 98) that ends ineligible and stands reserved after the clear.

The cleared candidate p = [0, 120, 2840, 5780, 1240, 20, 0, 0] lies strictly inside the band at every traded knot — between the beliefs by construction, so the published point inside each band is the submitter’s selection, not market revelation. The selection rule is stated, deterministic, and re-derived by the checked script: maximize settlement divisibility (which pins the $140 coordinate uniquely at 2,840 and sets the 50-lot size), then nearest-to-midpoint for the free knots, the widest remaining band absorbing the simplex sum, lowest index on ties. One constraint shaped this book and we say so plainly: the settlement seam refuses any nonzero rounding pot and any virtual leg (entitlement.rs:337–342), so every consideration had to be an exact integer of atoms — that is why the quotes are 500 Eggs, the package is 50 lots, and every pᵢ is divisible by 20. Of the 77 exact in-band simplex candidates, all 77 clear the ten single quotes strictly and 76 also clear the package pair; the one exception (p₁₄₀ = 2,960, where dot(c⁺,p) = 885,680 exceeds the conservatively-inward buy limit 880,000) is named rather than rounded away.

⟨degen⟩ Settled and marked to its own model, G is up 196.115 atoms; marked to its own model, E is up 289.11. Both sides think they won, and the two numbers sum to exactly the disagreement traded — 485.225 atoms. That’s not a bug in the exhibit; that is what a priced disagreement is. Nobody finds out who was right, because nothing resolves.

What actually executed

The whole book — thirteen orders: ten crossing singles, the package pair, the low-ball — placed through the general clearing plane’s real instruction tags (47–59) in a local Agave bank, froze, walked the candidate to its verdict through the streaming relation, selected, entitled per-slice receipts, and settled through Settle ×6: five entitled single crossings plus the portfolio full pair, seven pairing slices in all. Conservation was asserted across the whole value plane: final positions byte-equal to the allocation the accepted summary implies, total cash and per-outcome Eggs exactly the injected genesis, consumed reservations archived at their exact envelopes, the ineligible low-ball still standing ACTIVE at its 5-atom envelope, the rounding pot zero, and the release identity closed to the atom: 4,920 = 4,749 + 171 + 0. The three licenses, each at its true plane

Normalization is forced, not chosen. Partition of unity — the hats sum to one at every admissible value — is a machine-checked theorem about the emitted basis. PROVED-MODEL — CURRENT_TRUTH.md §3; lean/DragonsClutch/BSpline.lean Its pricing consequence is Theorem 3.1 of the research corpus: “every dual-feasible π satisfies Σᵢ πᵢ = S exactly” — the simplex sum is dual feasibility of the split/merge columns, a paper proof over the repo’s exact integer objects. paper proof — DUAL_IS_THE_MEASURE.md §3.2

Measure-hood. Theorem 7.1: “at degree ≤ 1, every vector the venue can ever publish (every V1-valid p) is the basis-moment vector of a probability measure, exhibited explicitly; and on the allocation-A accept set that same vector is a zero-gap optimal dual. The certificate and the measure are carried by one object.” The general theorem is a paper proof; this exhibit’s instance is elementary — degree-1 hats are cardinal, N ⱼ(tᵢ) = δᵢⱼ, so Q* = Σ (pᵢ/S)·δtᵢ represents p with EQ*[Nⱼ] = pⱼ/S exactly — and the check script proves the instance by direct arithmetic, all 64 pairs. paper proof — DUAL_IS_THE_MEASURE.md §7.2, Thm 7.1 instance checked — docs/site-plan/disagreement_check.py The implied forward $156.40 sits between the two models’ $156.24 and $156.70.

The certificate. Theorem 5.1: under the frozen policy tuple, every candidate the relation accepts is, with (π, s) := (p, s(p)), a zero-duality-gap certificate of surplus optimality for the clearing LP — the fills maximize batch surplus over the entire real LP polytope, and p is an optimal dual. Paper proof; and this exhibit’s gap is recomputed term by term in the script: every strict order fully filled, the ineligible low-ball at zero with zero slack, gap exactly 0, certified surplus 4,331,000 price units. paper proof — DUAL_IS_THE_MEASURE.md §5.1, Thm 5.1

⟨scholar⟩ Q* is a representing measure, never the measure: at degree 1 the venue pins the measure’s spline-dual coordinates and nothing finer, and within each no-trade band the published point is a stated selection rule, drawn shaded above rather than passed off as revelation. The zero gap is relative to the frozen tuple (DUAL_IS_THE_MEASURE.md §5.1, §7.2, §7.5).

What does not carry over

Said plainly, because the badges only mean something if this list is on the same page. The Lean instantiation of the certificate theorems at this exact matrix (CertF) is open, so none of the optimality or measure claims is machine-checked over these definitions — they are paper proofs with named falsifiers, and this page never upgrades them. The walk is bank evidence, unpromoted. The quantity is synthetic and nothing here touches value: the deposit boundary refuses by design. At degree ≥ 2 the measure reading is refuted — there are simplex-valid vectors that are no measure’s moments, with an explicit arbitrage (§7.4) — so this exhibit lives strictly on the proved degree-1 rung, where those vectors cannot exist. And the exhibit’s two traders are estimators run by the exhibit’s author, coordinated by a published function — not autonomous agents discovering each other in an order flow.

Falsify it. Run python3 docs/site-plan/disagreement_check.py — it re-derives the series from the seed, both estimators, the book, and every number on this page in exact arithmetic, and exits nonzero on any drift; move any pᵢ outside its band and the named strict inequality flips. Run the bank walk: cargo test --locked --test disagreement_exhibit under programs/clutch-sbf/svm-tests drives T0 and the full thirteen-order clear against the sealed runtime and fails on any broken conservation identity. The exhibit is exactly as strong as those two commands, and no stronger.
