SUBJECTS.  Capitalized imperative, no trailing period, typically 6-12 words, and
almost always carrying a *story* rather than a category — the verb is what
changed and the tail is why it matters:

    "Green the keeper gate: three more defects the real bank found"
    "Reattribute the compute ceiling from architecture to a software hasher"
    "Stop naming a degree-three supremum I did not derive"
    "Take custody on the default ELF for the first time"
    "Retire VirtualPot: pay, then mint, then deliver"
    "Say what binding the wrong degree does"

Frequent forms: colon-headline plus consequence; "Retire X" / "Close X" /
"Retract X" for obligations discharged; "Say X" / "Name X" / "Stop X-ing" for
prose that was over- or under-claiming.  Zero exact-duplicate subjects across
698 commits.  28 subjects begin lowercase; 22 of those use conventional-commit
prefixes (feat/fix/test/docs with a scope: `feat(sbf):`, `test(source):`,
`docs(svm):`) and all 22 sit inside one contiguous 08-18/08-19 band.

BODIES.  Hard-wrapped at ~72-76 columns, prose paragraphs, no bullet-only
bodies outside doc-sweep commits.  The canonical shape is four movements:

  1. What landed, in one dense paragraph naming the real objects and numbers.
  2. One paragraph per surprise, correction, or deviation, each stated against
     the thing it contradicts ("Design correction found by the oracle…",
     "Finding worth canon…", "The honest cap answer…", "One correction to the
     promotion report's rent story, found by the campaign rather than argued").
  3. A gates-and-counts line: "layout 83, reference 38, clutch-sbf 85+7, kernel
     16, vertical 19, batch 61, vector spine 25/25" — exact test counts and
     byte/CU figures, never "all tests pass."
  4. The honest boundary, unhedged: "HOST-TESTED only; no live tags, accounts,
     or instructions."  "Host-model code: tested, not verified, not the SVM
     relation."  "This is SBF-executed focused local-bank evidence for the
     sealed instruction set, nothing more."

CLAIM-PLANE VOCABULARY.  A small closed vocabulary is used with discipline and
never inflated: PROPOSED / MODEL-ONLY / DESIGN-ONLY / HOST-TESTED /
SBF-EXECUTED / MEASURED / UNPROMOTED / CHECKED-FINITE / STOP / retired /
recorded residual / declared-permanent.  Measurements are labelled MEASURED and
distinguished from arguments; several commits say so explicitly ("measured, not
argued", "observed rather than argued", "reported not tuned", "found not
chosen").

NEGATIVE RESULTS ARE FIRST-CLASS.  Refutations get their own commits and their
own subjects ("the decomposition is FALSE, with counterexamples"), retractions
are labelled as retractions and the superseded text is kept and labelled
historical rather than deleted, and several commits carry corrections to the
author's own earlier prose in the same voice ("two doc defects of my own
making", "my own truncated-log near-miss").
