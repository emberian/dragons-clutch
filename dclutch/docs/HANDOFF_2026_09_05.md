## 1. What this is, in one paragraph

dClutch is a market kernel on Solana devnet: a stranger founds a question
about a fact the chain can observe, in one of five shapes (Direct, General,
Dealer, Series, Structured), trades fully-backed claims with nothing borrowed
and nothing to liquidate, is resolved by an observation the market paid for
inside a window it declared, and is paid into an ordinary wallet's token
account. Every claim the site makes is derived from chain records; every
layout the programs enforce is stated first in Lean and emitted into the
bytes. `docs/INTENT.md` says what it is for and what it refuses (§4 — the
refusals are load-bearing; do not "fix" them). `README.md` says what the
chain shows today.

## 8. Pitfalls that cost real hours

- A cohort lane's scratch is load-bearing for its job dir; never remove it
  while the cohort is live (I did; it forced the release gate to bind to
  reproducible bytes, which was overdue, but cost a rebuild).
- `timeout N cmd | head` reports head's exit status; a killed process reads
  as clean. Capture the status inside the subshell before any pipe.
- Absent signal needs a positive control: "nothing fired" and "my
  instrument was disconnected" log identically.
- A count that falls by deletion is not evidence; report deltas as "by
  evidence" or "by deletion".
- Two authors of one fact is the recurring defect (the Series config
  identity had six sites and two values; the proof width had five authors).
  Fix by giving the fact one owner, never by aligning the copies.
- Lanes that end their turn inside a long wait resume on their own child's
  completion; a lane whose context filled cannot be resumed — hand off from
  the state on disk (this document is that state for you).
- zsh: `mapfile` does not exist; `for f in $list` does not split; quote
  collisions inside `bash -c '…'` heredocs fail silently — write a script
  file and run it.
