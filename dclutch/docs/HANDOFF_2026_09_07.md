## 1. What this is

dClutch is a market kernel on Solana devnet. A stranger founds a question about
a fact the chain can observe, in one of five families (Direct, General, Dealer,
Series, Structured); trades fully-backed claims, nothing borrowed and nothing to
liquidate; the market is resolved by an observation it paid for inside a window
it declared; and it pays into an ordinary wallet's token account. Every claim
the site makes is derived from chain records. Every layout the programs enforce
is stated in Lean first and emitted into the bytes.

- `docs/INTENT.md` — what it is for, and §4 the refusals. The refusals are
  load-bearing; do not "fix" them.
- `GOAL.md` — a ~110-line INDEX. Never append prose to it. Dated deltas go to
  `docs/ledger/<date>.md`, one row per day in the index.
- `AGENTS.md` — the rules a lane must follow. Read it before touching anything.
- `docs/MASTER_COMPLETION_CONTRACT.md` — rows C-00..C-17. C-16 (assurance) is
  the one that is not met.

## 3. How to work here

- **Commit** only through `tools/lane.sh commit <msg> -- <named paths>` (or
  `commit-patch`). Never `git add -A` in the live tree. Set `DCLUTCH_LANE=<you>`.
- **Publish** with `tools/cut.sh`. It mirrors the live tree's HEAD into
  `/Users/ember/dev/dragons-clutch` (subtree `dclutch/`) and pushes. It now
  refuses unless HEAD is `main`, because a cut published a lane's branch on
  2026-09-07; `CUT_ANY_BRANCH=1` is the deliberate override, and `LIVE=<path>`
  lets you cut from a worktree when a lane holds the shared checkout.
- **Gates**: `tools/gate --list` is the one table. `tools/gate cheap` is the
  routine sweep. Currently red and owned by nobody: `seam` (21–28 findings),
  `clippy` (five packages), and see §4.
- **Builds**: SBF program builds and any campaign go on **hbox** under
  `swarm-build` in `/tank`, never natively (the canonical builder is
  platform-tools v1.53 on Linux/x86_64; a macOS build is not the release's
  bytes). Disk on this laptop has hard-faulted once; one target dir per
  worktree, never shared (a shared one INVENTS errors across siblings).
- **Devnet** acts are standing-authorized; mainnet, tags, releases and
  force-push are not. The Helius key is at `~/.helius-key`, read at use time and
  never written into a file.

## 5. The laws that make this tree work

One author per fact — most walls this week were two authors of one number.
Every claim generated, or dated and owned. A refusal names its conjunct; a
`map_err(|_| Coarse)` is the tree's signature defect. Codes live under decision
0007's bands and are never renumbered once shipped; a retired code becomes a
withdrawn variant, and a band asserts a contiguous run, so a hole is a compile
error. Lean-first for any emitted layout: ABI module, emitter, guard — and the
guard must actually pass, because two proof files were found this week that had
never been built. A test asserts an exact named refusal, never `is_err()`.
Delete by reading, with a census control.

## 6. Pitfalls paid for in real hours

- A control re-asserted at every step stops being a measurement (two crates were
  carried as "red on main" through four landings after being fixed).
- A harness that SEEDS a prestate agrees with any order; ask which stages a walk
  actually drove.
- `timeout N cmd | head` reports head's status, so a killed process reads clean.
- A maker's own handoff doc is a claim, not evidence; check it against its diff.
- A worktree shared by a lane and its own children has no single author.
- Absent signal needs a positive control: "nothing fired" and "my instrument was
  disconnected" log identically.
