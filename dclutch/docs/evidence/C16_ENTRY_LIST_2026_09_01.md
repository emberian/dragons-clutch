# C-16 entry list — what remains before independent hostile review can begin

Status: gate list, not a status board. Owner: lane S11 (C-00, C-16).
Measured at `cce8705f`, 2026-09-01. Companion: `docs/evidence/DEBT_OWNERSHIP_LEDGER_2026_09_01.md`.

C-16 says reviewers may start only when "no known material gap, unexplained
authority, stale claim, unowned economic flow, never-executed intended route, or
user-inaccessible capability remains." This is the list of what does remain, by
that row's own six categories.

Every item is in exactly one of two states, per
`docs/MASTER_COMPLETION_CONTRACT.md`: **implemented with evidence**, or **ruled
out by Ember with a date**. Nothing here is deferred, parked, or out of scope.
Where an item is neither yet, it says so and names who owns making it one.

This list is short on purpose. It is meant to be true, not complete-looking; a
category with no instrument says so rather than reporting zero.

## 0. Read this before the list

**Two of C-16's six categories have no instrument at all.** Nothing in the tree
measures *unexplained authority* or *unowned economic flow* — the only place
either phrase appears is the contract row that demands them. Four categories
below carry numbers; two carry an honest blank. A reviewer should treat those
two as unswept ground, not as clean ground (§5, §6).

**The register that answers three of the four is young and was wrong until this
week.** `docs/reference/routes.md` derives route status from
`tools/gauntlet/*/bindings.json` alone and **never consults the evidence
ledger**: it cannot distinguish a route whose chain evidence was folded from one
whose campaign has never run. Corroboration lives in `dclutch-route-census
observe` → `CENSUS.md`. Where the two disagree, **CENSUS.md is the evidence and
`routes.md` is the claim**. A reviewer who cites a "witnessed" row is citing an
assertion.

**`docs/reference/` is stale at HEAD and is being held deliberately.** The
convergence owner is holding regeneration until the lanes quiesce, so in-tree
reference numbers lag this file. The numbers here are freshly measured; the
published ones are not yet.

## 8. What a hostile reviewer should distrust, and why

These are the epistemics, not the findings. They are here because a reviewer
who does not know them will repeat the errors this campaign made.

### 8.1 A dead code has at least three causes, and the census names none of them

One instance of each appeared in this campaign:

| cause | instance | what it turned out to be |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the route was never built | `0x5006 CustodyRequired` | genuinely dead; R-3 |
| the guard was deleted with its route | the ten `LiabilityBasisSbfErrorV2` codes | withdrawn with `DCLLBX02`, which had no producer and no chain record, so not one was ever raised |
| **the guard was present and I could not see it** | `0x5644 Phase` | **I convicted it as a missing guard. It was reachable, and S7 landed the real one.** |

**The census reports absence; it never reports cause.** A census whose output is
treated as a verdict produces exactly the third row. Every "dead" finding needs
a second method before it becomes an accusation.

### 8.2 The shape to look for: a present test, hollowed out

The sharpest instance anyone found this campaign is not a missing test.
`crates/dclutch-svm-harness`'s `resolution_successor` campaign had
`primary_instruction` and `funded_caller_instruction` replaced by `panic!` stubs
at 2026-08-26 00:19 (`d1325c7f`); the `#[ignore]` landed **47 minutes later**
(`583e5bfa`). The body was unreachable for six days **while the README went on
quoting its ten-row compute table as evidence**. Found and remediated by the
resolution lane (`svm-harness/README.md:97-109`, `WAVE.md:2329-2342`).

**Not a missing test, but a present one hollowed out while its own documentation
kept citing it.** A reviewer should assume this shape exists elsewhere and look
for it directly: green suites whose bodies do not run, and prose that cites
them.

### 8.3 The instruments were wrong four times, and each was found by re-deriving

Every number in this file comes from tooling that was wrong at least once:
prefix shadowing in an `rg -F` alternation (would have reported 30 dead refusal
codes instead of 12); leftmost-match truncation on `crate::Enum::Variant`;
a filename scan that missed `*-bindings.json` (reported two bound campaigns as
unbound); and a census-inventory check that reported "no ELF dispatches two of
them" for all ten magic collisions, because the inventory records those as
`Predicate` selectors rather than `Magic` — a **false negative** on the exact
question being asked.

The rule that caught all four: **an absent signal is evidence only when
something present proves the channel works.** Every claim of absence in this
file was checked by a second method that shares none of the first's
assumptions, and the route register's own control is that a route known to
execute (`claims/founding_v5::process`) reads witnessed through the same path.

### 8.4 The instrument catches its own author

`a19d93b1` moved a wire discriminant (`RecordActionV1::Begin`, `1` → `5`) and
left tier 1's bindings pointing at the old route id — **the identical defect
this campaign convicted in `fdfbe0dd` two days earlier**, committed by the lane
that convicted it, four commits later. It was caught within the hour by the
stale-binding-ref check added for the first instance, and repaired in
`cce8705f`.

Recorded because it is the strongest available argument for the check: the
author who had just written the finding still made the mistake. A reviewer
should assume the same of every convention in this tree that is enforced by
attention rather than by a gate.
