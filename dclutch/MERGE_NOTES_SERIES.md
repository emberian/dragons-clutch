### Rulings (provisional; ember rules by reversal)

1. **The Series lifecycle has no devnet path, therefore it has no cohort runbook rows.** The
   branch shipped `devnet-series-{open,consume,expire}-v1` and four `steps.tsv` rows driving them
   with `--cluster devnet`, plus a stub that refused under exactly that cluster. Read from the
   campaign outwards, the stub understates it: `authenticate_series_terminal_campaign_input_v1`
   refuses any `rpcUrl` that is not `http://127.0.0.1:` *as a conjunct*; the input schema is
   spelled `dclutch-owned-loopback-series-terminal-campaign-input-v2`; and **nothing in this tree
   writes that input** — `SeriesHotAcquisitionRecipeV2` and `SeriesCurrentSourceCorpusV1` are
   declared once each, as `Deserialize`-only readers inside the campaign. No arrangement of
   clusters makes those rows run, so building the ledger identity's devnet form would have been
   the third layer of a four-layer stack whose first layer is missing.
   `tools/gauntlet/magic-collisions.json` already recorded the first half of this fact.
   - The three verbs stay — fixing the act *is* their content, and it is a real safety property —
     renamed for the campaign and cluster they reach:
     `local-private-validator-series-terminal-{prepare,consume,expire}-v1`, in
     `series_act_verbs_v1.rs`. `--cluster` goes, and the devnet stub with it: a flag offering a
     value the campaign refuses is a second author of which chain this is.
   - `SeriesCampaignPolicyV1` keeps `expected_act` and loses `cluster`.
   - The four `steps.tsv` rows go. **This is also the least this branch can hand the merge
     queue**: `build/founder-bond` and `build/claims-split-merge` both insert rows in that file,
     and this one no longer touches it at all.
2. **The Series Shadow certificate has no author in this tree, of either binding.** Ruling 2 of
   the BUILD doc (the certificate binds `semantic_release_id`, never the ELF digest) stands and
   the acquisition admits both bindings. What does not stand is its seam: the generator does not
   *author* a certificate, it carries the identity its source manifest states
   (`generator/src/manifest.rs`, `CERTIFICATE_IDENTITY`; `build_series_shadow_source_v1` reads
   `input.checked_release.certificate`), and the only in-tree writer of one is
   `series_shadow_lab_include`, which takes it on the command line and says of itself that it is
   not release evidence.
3. **`0xC200` keeps one discriminant and gains a `msg!`.** Four Trading causes (`Release`,
   `Content`, `ReleaseSuperseded`, `DeploymentSlotMismatch`) reach the wire as one accelerator
   code. Splitting it into four is the stronger repair and is a decision-0007 change with a band
   table three branches contend for; the cause survives on the refusing path instead.
4. Ruling 4 of the BUILD doc (**one Series operator path, V5; the three V3 builders deleted
   rather than given callers**) is upheld. The original brief asked for callers; AGENTS.md's "do
   not preserve parallel legacy/current authority paths" and the fact that `inspect_current_series_hot_v5`
   has three live callers make deletion the honest build. The one thing that deletion cost —
   a Series request round-trip proof — is replaced and widened (§4).
