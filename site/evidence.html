How this project says true things

The evidence

Every badge on this site resolves here. The vocabulary below is the repository’s own, verbatim, and it is deliberately nontransitive: no label ever substitutes for another, a green model never impersonates runtime evidence, and a proved theorem sitting next to an executed instruction does not make the instruction proved (CURRENT_TRUTH.md §1). “Implemented” is considered too broad a word for this control plane; every promoted claim names its plane instead.

One further marker appears on this site: paper proof. The project’s research documents prove theorems pen-and-paper over the repository’s exact integer objects and label them “PROVED HERE” — with the explicit caveat that nothing may be called verified until the pinned proof toolchain closes it over the actual definitions, and with named falsifiers gating any language upgrade (docs/research/DUAL_IS_THE_MEASURE.md §0, §11; docs/research/RISK_SUMMED_POSITIONS.md, status note). A paper proof is MODEL-or-lower in the vocabulary above, and this site badges it as itself, never as a machine-checked result.

⟨degen⟩ The badge is the trust dial. “Runs in laboratory bank evidence” is not “live on mainnet,” a proved theorem is not a working program, and this site is built so it cannot blur that line even where blurring would read better.
⟨builder⟩ The labels are CURRENT_TRUTH.md §1 verbatim, and they gate commit language: promotion means crossing a named gate with artifacts produced by the final joined bytes, never relabeling (CURRENT_TRUTH.md §7).
⟨scholar⟩ Nontransitivity is the point: the planes do not compose. PROVED-MODEL plus HOST-TESTED does not entail a correct executable — that entailment is a refinement theorem, which either exists as its own named object (the CHECKED-FINITE bridge, the Verus subset) or does not exist at all (CURRENT_TRUTH.md §1, §3).

What the strongest evidence looks like

The proof story is carried by Lean: 184 theorems at zero sorry, reporting only Lean’s three standard axioms (propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound); the B-spline file alone holds 159 counted declarations, 116 of them theorems — clamped rational constructions, exact split distances, constructive and unique largest-remainder selection, local support, solvency, and the complete-set results (docs/reviews/PLANNED_VS_BUILT_2026-08-19.md; CURRENT_TRUTH.md §3; lean/DragonsClutch/). PROVED-MODEL

The bridge from model to executable is finite and honest about it: eight Lean-computed fixtures agree byte-for-byte with the digest-pinned production evaluator, five mutations of the actual Rust source compile, execute, and go red, and a 34,766-case independent Python differential reruns alongside (CURRENT_TRUTH.md §3, campaign be8eba3). CHECKED-FINITE The artifact discipline seals exact bytes: repeated ordinary builds of the runtime are byte-identical on the sealing host; an independent second-OS rebuild diverged and the divergence was exhaustively classified — two toolchain CI path strings, one reordered prebuilt intrinsics cluster, an eight-byte shift, zero bytes derived from source (CURRENT_TRUTH.md §2). The checked evidence manifest executed all 100 of its declared gates, and the most recent independent second-host attestation re-ran the portable evidence from a fresh archive: 44/44 gates PASS, 0 STOP, every file compared twice, the sealed ELF byte-verified in six contexts (CURRENT_TRUTH.md §2; GOAL.md done-log, Cycle D). None of that is a release, a deployment, or an external audit — the tree says so in the same breath, every time.

What honesty looks like when it hurts

For weeks the record said on-chain re-verification of a clearing “does not fit”: the measured route consumed exactly 1,400,000 compute units — the whole ceiling — and rolled back. The measurement was right. The conclusion was wrong, and the corpus caught its own error: the cost was one dependency edge compiling in a fully unrolled software SHA-256 — a single 53,952-byte symbol. Routing hashing through the runtime’s syscall made every measured instruction 3–8× cheaper, and the same selection route now completes and commits at 226,071 CU (docs/reviews/COMPUTE_CEILING_REATTRIBUTION_2026-08-19.md). SBF-EXECUTED · measured The part worth pointing at: the wrong verdict was not deleted. It stands in the record, labeled, next to its correction, with the list of documents the correction invalidates. Sites don’t usually show you their wrong answers; this project’s correction process is the product.

The two-ELF discipline

The system is built to make its own incompleteness machine-visible. The sealed default artifact refuses deposits with the typed error SourceReleaseUnavailable (0x79) because its production source registry is empty — no real data source exists yet, so the program will not accept value, and that refusal is asserted per degree in the joined lifecycle campaigns (CURRENT_TRUTH.md §4, §6.1). SBF-EXECUTED · refusal asserted Anything that succeeds against mock infrastructure must run on a distinct, explicitly non-production ELF; the mock provider’s account body is the literal string MOCK-PROVIDER-V1, and the project’s own assessment names the seam without flinching: “the joins are the fiction” (docs/reviews/SOPHISTICATION_GAP_2026-08-19.md §1). That sentence is why the badges on this site mean something — the corpus polices the line between what runs and what pretends, harder than any outsider does.

One production note in the same spirit: this site is static files with no RPC, no wallet, no analytics, and no operator — the posture the project’s own client design mandates, in which any frontend is a replaceable lens and never part of consensus (docs/STATIC_CLIENT.md).

Published artifacts

Some exhibits are small enough to publish whole. The manipulation-cost table (v1) is the 1,080-row deterministic cost surface computed across candidate settlement-window designs — fully synthetic parameter tuples, exact integer arithmetic, no market data — including the run whose result refuted a claim in an earlier draft of the author’s own regulatory comment before any human read it. The header of the file describes its own columns. A computable design can be wrong in public, and corrected; this file is that argument’s exhibit, correction scar and all. Its sequel, the off-hours sweep (v2), adds a session-thinness dimension — 8,640 rows asking how each settlement-window design’s manipulation cost survives a thin overnight session. The finding: a window contained in the thin session loses its cost linearly with depth; the widest clawback any surveyed design achieves is sixfold; no design in the grid holds its daylight cost at a tenth of normal depth. Two honesty rails ride in the file’s own header: a ratio above par never means the thin session is more protected (the cheapest attack is the minimum across sessions), and the model deliberately forbids carrying inventory across liquidity’s return — a strategy that, if allowed, can make the attack profitable — so these rows must never be read as proving straddling windows protective.

A second checkable claim from the same laboratory banks: terminal closure repays recorded principal exactly. Every program account in the general clearing plane records its rent payer at creation, and closing it returns exactly the recorded principal to exactly that payer, with any surplus routed to a burn sink rather than to anyone’s discretion. Asserted to the lamport across a cleared epoch’s full machinery: 531,639,600 of 531,652,377 lamports reclaimed; the 12,777-lamport difference is exactly two deliberately injected test donations, and the residual is one 64-byte policy artifact that is permanent by design. This executed in local test banks (SBF-EXECUTED, unpromoted, like everything else here); who funded which account, and who was repaid, is itself a kept, checkable record.

A third exhibit is checkable end to end: the disagreement exhibit (The Disagreement). Two named textbook estimators, run once on a published synthetic series under a pre-registered seed rule, emitted densities over the same eight-knot degree-1 basis; a published deterministic book-former lowered the pair into a 13-order book that placed, froze, cleared, and settled end to end through the general clearing plane in a laboratory bank, conservation asserted to the atom and the pot provably empty (SBF-EXECUTED · bank · UNPROMOTED; programs/clutch-sbf/svm-tests/tests/disagreement_exhibit.rs, executed against the exact sealed runtime). Every number the page shows is re-derived from the seed and the published rules by docs/site-plan/disagreement_check.py, which exits nonzero on any drift — including one correction the script forced on the design prose it implements: of the 77 exact in-band candidates, exactly 76 clear the disagreement package, and the script names the one corner that does not. No resolution step exists; neither model is ever “right.”

Scope

Scope. Dragon’s Clutch is a research prototype. Nothing is deployed; no market, token, or deployment carries real value, and none is offered. The clearing plane and smooth-claim lifecycle you read about here executed in local test banks and remain UNPROMOTED in the project’s own liveness profile; fees are forced to zero everywhere; the default build refuses deposits (0x79) because no production data source exists yet; no external security audit, signed release, or legal determination exists. The mathematics pages distinguish machine-checked theorems from paper proofs from measured executions — follow any badge. Nothing on this site is an offer, a solicitation, financial advice, or a statement that any regulator has approved anything.

That is the entire hedge. The badges carry local truth; this note carries global scope; the prose everywhere else is confident because it can afford to be.
