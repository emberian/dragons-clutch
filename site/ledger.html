Conservation, resolution, and walking to zero

The ledger

One identity rules custody

Everything a market holds obeys one equation. Write H for the actual collateral atoms in the market’s Hoard token account, L for retained claim backing, P for the sum of every Position’s cash, and S for unsolicited surplus someone donated without being asked. Then:

H = L + P + S

Every transition the program can perform has an exact delta against this identity — not “approximately,” not “after fees,” but to the atom (CURRENT_TRUTH.md §5). Only two transitions move actual tokens across the Hoard’s edge: Endow inbound and WithdrawCash outbound. Everything between — splitting, merging, trading, redeeming internally — is pooled-accounting reclassification inside the vault.

Settlement under the identity

When a cleared epoch settles, the identity is not assumed — it is asserted, plane-wide, on the bank’s actual bytes: cash sums exact to the atom, per-outcome position totals exact, the release identity closed (buy envelopes = consideration + refunds + pot), and the final Positions byte-equal to the verified summary’s implied allocation. Entitlement receipts are the consumption latch: each one frozen from the selected candidate before any value moves, and consumed exactly once, which is what makes settlement resumable without ever being repeatable. Resolution is a measurement

Every transition before resolution preserves the structure of risk — split, merge, transfer, materialize all move value around without creating or destroying any of it. Resolution is the one exception, and the corpus treats it with the respect a physicist gives a measurement: the risk space collapses to a point, positions that differed in shape become economically identical, and the collateral requirement can only fall, never rise (docs/research/RISK_SUMMED_POSITIONS.md §1.5). For the Friday clutch the admitted statistic lands at $163.40, between the $160 and $180 knots. The hats evaluate to exact rationals — 53.12/64 and 10.88/64 — and the one deterministic rounding rule, largest remainder with lowest-index ties, distributes the last atoms: the payout vector is (0, 0, 0, 53, 11, 0, 0, 0) of 64. Same inputs, same vector, every time, on every machine — the rule is proved canonical and unique in the Lean model. PROVED-MODEL — CURRENT_TRUTH.md §3; lean/DragonsClutch/BSpline.lean

Redemption is exact-or-refuse

Fractional resolved weights make some redemptions inexact, and the program’s answer to “almost” is no. A lot that does not divide exactly returns RemainderRequired before any mutation; nothing is silently rounded against you, ever (CURRENT_TRUTH.md §3). The balanced exit is always available: a complete set redeems at par, exactly.

⟨degen⟩ No haircut hidden in a rounding step. If your lot doesn’t divide, the venue refuses instead of shaving you — bring a whole lot or merge to a complete set and exit at par.
⟨builder⟩ Settlement asserts conservation on reloaded account bytes, not on the driver’s bookkeeping: cash sums, per-outcome totals, and final Positions are compared byte-for-byte against the verified summary’s implied allocation (GOAL.md T2-8; CURRENT_TRUTH.md §4, clearing row).
⟨scholar⟩ Pre-resolution transitions are isometries of a seminormed space with a conserved quotient class; resolution composes with the evaluation functional — a rank-one projection, the unique non-isometry — and requirements degrade monotonically through it (RISK_SUMMED_POSITIONS.md §1.4–1.5, Prop 8).

The walk to zero

The strongest custody evidence in the tree is a story with an ending: 22 signed, sequential transactions on a local validator — create the market, endow, split, materialize, transfer to a bearer, merge, advance the feed, resolve, redeem the winners, redeem the losers for zero, and withdraw everything — including two expected refusals confirmed on-chain that changed not one watched byte. 18 watched accounts were reloaded after every step. The 70 deposited atoms closed exactly: 3 to the bearer, 61 to the founder, 6 to the second owner, and every terminal balance — Position cash, locked backing, the pooled Hoard token account — ended at zero. SBF-EXECUTED — docs/implementation/COMMITTED_SBF_WALK.md; CURRENT_TRUTH.md §2 And after the green run, the harness corrupted one byte of the final expected Hoard image and replayed the whole sequence: the gate failed, specifically, on committed bytes. A gate that cannot go red proves nothing; this one went red on demand, which is what makes its green mean something.

The Friday clutch · stage 4 of 7 · settled and resolved

Settlement moves Maya’s 1,728 atoms to Theo and the tent’s Eggs to Maya, under the asserted identity. The week ends; the TWAP resolves at $163.40; the payout vector is (0,0,0,53,11,0,0,0)/64. Maya redeems her position — 3,200 Eggs at $160 pay 2,650; 1,600 at $180 pay 275 — 2,925 atoms in all, exactly, no remainder. Theo’s complement redeems for 275. Payouts exhaust the locked backing to zero: 2,925 + 275 = 3,200. Both withdraw — 3,197 and 2,003 — and the Hoard token account reads zero. Maya is up 1,197 atoms; Theo is down the same 1,197; the ledger neither made nor lost an atom.
